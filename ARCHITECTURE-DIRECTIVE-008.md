# Architecture Directive 008 — Learn Once. Use Everywhere.

Copyright © 2026 Istockhomes®  
All rights reserved.

---

## The Law

A member who learns one part of Istockhomes should instinctively know how to use every other part.

The platform may grow forever.

The learning curve should not.

---

## Core Principles

### One Navigation

The primary navigation remains familiar regardless of industry.

A hotel, artist, marina, restaurant, aircraft company, gallery, creator, or realtor should all feel like they belong to the same platform.

---

### Familiar Modules

Modules evolve independently.

Examples include:

- Gallery
- Reviews
- Live
- Marketplace
- Booking
- Events
- Team
- Contact
- Map

New capabilities are added inside modules whenever practical instead of creating entirely new navigation patterns.

---

### Consistency Before Creativity

Businesses express their identity through their content, photography, branding, and stories.

The platform expresses its identity through consistency.

Creativity should enhance the experience, not make it harder to use.

---

### Learn Once

A member should never have to relearn:

- Search
- Maps
- Messaging
- Reviews
- Booking
- Marketplace
- Business Pages

Learning one experience should transfer naturally across every business category.

---

### Innovation Happens Inside the Framework

Innovation is encouraged.

However, innovation should occur inside established modules and workflows whenever possible.

The steering wheel should never move.

---

## The Design Test

Every proposed feature must answer one question before implementation:

**Does this make the platform easier to learn or more confusing?**

If it makes the platform more confusing, it should be redesigned or integrated into an existing pattern.

Walter may ask:

**Does this fit the platform, or does it make users learn a new platform?**

---

## Closing Statement

People should spend their time discovering businesses—not discovering how to use the platform.
